# Catalog Transcription Ambiguities

## Unverified Chapter Card Faces

- Status: blocked on source material.
- Affected data: every field in all 69 records in `cards.json` that is specific
  to a physical card face: printed coin and Skill costs, choice Skills, chaining
  requirements and symbols, Race symbols, and effect values/regions.
- Why: `rules.pdf` describes card families and general mechanics on pp. 4-5 but
  does not show or list individual card faces. `helpsheet.pdf` likewise contains
  only Landmark, quest, and Alliance-reference material.
- Required evidence: a scan/photo of each Chapter-card face, or an official
  component list/reference that maps the face to its Chapter and printed icons.
- Decision: retain existing transcription. Do not infer changes from family
  patterns or the original 7 Wonders Duel.

## Card Names

- Status: not applicable with the supplied source material.
- The supplied rulebook and player aid give no textual names for Chapter cards.
  The catalog deliberately uses stable physical IDs rather than invented names.

## Confirmed Correction

- `barad_dur` display name changed from `Barad Dur` to `Barad-Dûr`.
